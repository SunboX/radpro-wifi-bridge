RadPro Detector Update

Update the connected detector to Rad Pro 3.1.1. Requires stable Rad Pro 3.0.2 or later (3.x) on Bosean FS-600, FS-1000, FS-5000 or GQ GMC-800.

Download the official firmware file

Download the file above, then select it here. The bridge checks its model, size and SHA-256 before entering the bootloader.

Keep the detector and bridge powered and connected throughout the update. Measurements pause during flashing. An interrupted flash may need recovery with the upstream installer. Settings and log pages are not deliberately erased; back up important data first.

Other devices and recovery

FNIRSI GC-01, JOY-IT JT-RAD01 and GC-03 use vendor USB drive bootloaders; the upstream guides require Windows. FS2011 and YT-203B need an external programmer. These methods are not implemented by this bridge.
